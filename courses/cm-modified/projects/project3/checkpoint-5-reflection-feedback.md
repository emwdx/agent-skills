# Checkpoint 5 — Peer Feedback and Reflection

**Topic:** Earthquakes
**Focus:** Collaboration — Being Part of the Class Community
**Proficiency Target:** Collaboration L3

---

## What You Are Doing

Even though you worked solo, you are still part of the class.
In this checkpoint, you will look at two other students' projects during the showcase.
You will write feedback for one of them.
Then you will reflect on how your own project went.

### Part D — Written Peer Feedback

**Step 9.** Choose one of the two students from Part C to give written feedback to.

**Step 10.** Write your feedback using the frame below.

> **To:** ___________________________________________
>
> Something I liked about your project was: ___________________________________________
> because: ___________________________________________.
>
> One design decision you made that was different from mine was: ___________________________________________
> I think you did this because: ___________________________________________.
>
> One suggestion I have is: ___________________________________________

**Here is an example:**

> **To:** Maria
>
> Something I liked about your project was: the colors you used on your website
> because: they made the warning signs easy to see right away.
>
> One design decision you made that was different from mine was: you used a video instead of a quiz
> I think you did this because: some people learn better by watching than by reading.
>
> One suggestion I have is: add a short summary at the top so people know what your project is about before they start.
