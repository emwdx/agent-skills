# Checkpoint 1 — Research Audit

**Topic:** Earthquakes
**Focus:** Adaptive Thinking — Learning from an Organization
**Proficiency Target:** Critical Thinking L3

---

## What You Are Doing

You are going to pick one real organization that helps protect people from earthquakes.
You will read about what they do and fill in a research chart.
You will write one paragraph explaining their point of view.

You are not doing a live interview.
You will find all your information online or in documents.

---

## Class Days for This Checkpoint: Days 1–2

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Part A — Pick Your Organization

**Step 1.** Read the list of organizations below.

| Organization | What They Do |
|---|---|
| USGS Earthquake Hazards Program | Scientists who study earthquakes and warn people |
| Japan Meteorological Agency | Runs Japan's earthquake early warning system |
| GeoNet (New Zealand) | Monitors earthquakes and sends alerts |
| Architects/Engineers for Seismic Safety (AESS) | Designs safer buildings in earthquake zones |
| American Red Cross — Earthquake Preparedness | Helps communities prepare for earthquakes |

**Step 2.** Choose one organization from the list above.

Write its name here:

> I chose: ___________________________________________

**Step 3.** Go to the organization's website or a public report about them.
(Your teacher can help you find the link if you need it.)

---

### Part B — Mission Analysis

**Step 4.** Read the organization's mission statement.
(The mission statement is usually on the "About Us" page.)

**Step 5.** Fill in this sentence frame:

> This organization's mission is to ___________________________.
> One thing they care about is ___________________________.
> One group of people they help is ___________________________.

**Here is an example of a completed sentence frame:**

> This organization's mission is to monitor earthquakes and warn people before shaking arrives.
> One thing they care about is giving people enough time to get to safety.
> One group of people they help is schools and hospitals in earthquake zones.

---

### Part C — Knowledge Audit Chart

**Step 6.** Fill in the chart below.
Read through the organization's website or report.
Write 3 things in each column.

| What I Know (from reading) | What I Would Like to Know |
|---|---|
| 1. | 1. |
| 2. | 2. |
| 3. | 3. |

**Here is an example of a completed row:**

| What I Know | What I Would Like to Know |
|---|---|
| The USGS sends earthquake alerts to phones in the U.S. | How far in advance do people actually get the warning? |

---

### Part D — The Organization's Point of View

**Step 7.** Read back through what you wrote in Part B and Part C.

**Step 8.** Write one paragraph using the frame below.
Fill in each blank with your own words.

> **[Organization name]** is trying to solve the problem of ___________________________.
> They believe this is a problem because ___________________________.
> The people most affected by this problem are ___________________________.
> This organization thinks the best way to help is ___________________________.

---

## You Are Done When:

- [ ] You have written the name of your organization in Step 2.
- [ ] You have filled in all three sentences in Part B.
- [ ] You have filled in at least 3 rows in the Knowledge Audit chart.
- [ ] You have filled in all four blanks in the Part D paragraph.

---

## Proficiency Check — Critical Thinking L3

Your teacher will use these three things to check your work:

1. You selected one claim from your reading and matched it to evidence from the organization's documents.
2. You completed a sentence stem describing one thing you still do not know.
3. You wrote a paragraph with two specific reasons why this organization matters.

If all four blanks in Part D are filled in with specific information (not just "they help people"), you are at L3.
