# Checkpoint 3 — AI Prototype

**Topic:** Earthquakes
**Focus:** Creativity — Building a Digital Tool
**Proficiency Target:** Creativity L3

---

## What You Are Doing

You are going to use Gemini and Canvas to build a simple digital tool about earthquakes.
The tool could help people learn about earthquakes, prepare for them, or understand how buildings stay safe.

You will write a prompt for Gemini, build the tool, and then make two improvements to it.

---

## Class Days for This Checkpoint: Days 5–6

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Part A — Plan Your Tool

Before you open Gemini, decide what your tool will do.

**Step 1.** Choose one idea from the list below.
Put a checkmark next to the one you pick.

- [ ] A quiz that tests what people know about earthquake safety
- [ ] A checklist for building an earthquake emergency kit at home
- [ ] A website page that explains how engineers design earthquake-safe buildings
- [ ] An interactive guide that shows what to do during and after an earthquake
- [ ] My own idea (describe it here): ___________________________________________

**Step 2.** Fill in this planning frame:

> My tool is called: ___________________________________________
>
> My tool will help people: ___________________________________________
>
> When someone uses it, they will: ___________________________________________

**Here is an example:**

> My tool is called: Earthquake Safety Quiz
>
> My tool will help people: learn what to do before, during, and after an earthquake
>
> When someone uses it, they will: answer five questions and see if their answers are right

---

### Part B — Write Your Gemini Prompt

You are going to write a prompt that tells Gemini exactly what to build.
A good prompt answers three questions.

**Step 3.** Answer each question below.

> **Question 1 — What does the tool do?**
> My tool: ___________________________________________

> **Question 2 — What does the screen look like?**
> The screen has: ___________________________________________

> **Question 3 — What happens when someone uses it?**
> When someone clicks / reads / answers, they: ___________________________________________

**Step 4.** Copy the three answers above into one prompt.
Use the format below.
Add the technical requirement line at the end exactly as written.

> I want you to build a tool called [name of your tool].
> It will help people [what it does].
> The screen should have [what it looks like].
> When someone uses it, [what happens].
>
> **Please write code in the Canvas that I can use to try out the app. Use local storage, not firebase.**

**Step 5.** Open Gemini.

**Step 6.** Paste your prompt into Gemini and press send.

**Step 7.** Wait for Gemini to generate the code in Canvas.

**Step 8.** Open Canvas and try out your tool.

---

### Part C — Iteration Log

After you try your tool, you will make two improvements.
Each improvement is called an iteration.

**Step 9.** Look at your tool and find one thing to change.

Fill in the first row:

| Iteration | What I asked Gemini to change | Why I wanted to change it | What it looks like after the change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | |
| 2 | | | |

**Here is an example of a completed row:**

| Iteration | What I asked Gemini to change | Why I wanted to change it | What it looks like after the change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Make the text bigger | It was too small to read easily | The text is now large enough to read on a tablet |

**Step 10.** Type your change request into Gemini.
Wait for the updated code.

**Step 11.** Try the updated tool.

**Step 12.** Fill in the second row in your Iteration Log.
Repeat Step 10 and Step 11 for your second change.

---

## You Are Done When:

- [ ] You have a checkmark next to your tool idea in Step 1.
- [ ] You have filled in all three blanks in the planning frame (Step 2).
- [ ] You have answered all three questions in Step 3.
- [ ] You have pasted your prompt into Gemini and received code.
- [ ] Both rows in the Iteration Log are filled in.

## Proficiency Check — Creativity L3

Your teacher will use these three things to check your work:

1. You selected one idea and completed the guided planning template explaining what it is and how it helps solve the problem.
2. You built a simple prototype using a step-by-step checklist (this document).
3. You used the feedback form (Iteration Log) and completed the sentence: "One way I could improve this is…"

**Bonus sentence frame — fill this in after your two iterations:**

> One way I could still improve this tool is: ___________________________________________

If your tool runs, your Iteration Log has two completed rows, and your bonus sentence is filled in, you are at L3.
