# Creativity Rubric: Dog Smelling Station Project

## Goal
This rubric shows how you show your creative thinking for the "dog smelling station" project. 

## Selection Criteria (L3)
To earn a high score, you must meet these three requirements:

### 1. Core Idea Identification
You have chosen one clear idea to share with others.
- [ ] The student has selected the "dog smelling station" as their primary project idea.
- [ ] The student can explain what it is using this frame:
  *My project is a dog smelling station because \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.*

### 2. Purpose and Problem Solving
You explained how your idea helps people in the counseling office.
- [ ] The student completed this sentence to explain the goal:
  *This project will help people by \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.*

### 3. Prototype Completion
You created a visual plan for your idea.
- [ ] The student has finished their Canva presentation as their "digital prototype."
- [ ] The student can show at least one specific feature of the station (e.g., how it smells or where it sits).
