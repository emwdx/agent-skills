# Day 3: Final Check and Practice

## Goal
You will check your presentation for mistakes and practice speaking in front of a friend or family member.

## Preparation
*   Have your Canva project open.
*   Have your scripts from Day 2 ready.

## Instructions
Follow each step below. Mark the checkbox when you are done with that step.

1. Check your Canva slides for spelling mistakes.
   - [ ] Done

2. Check your Canva slides to make sure the text is easy to read (large font).
   - [ ] Done

3. Look at your pictures in Canva. Make sure every picture has a label or title.
   - [ ] Done

4. Read your script out loud while looking at your presentation.
   - [ ] Done

5. Practice speaking your script to one person (like a friend, a parent, or a teacher).
   - [ ] Done

## Finish Line
You are finished when you have checked your spelling and font size, labeled your pictures, and practiced in front of one other person.
