# Day 1: Reviewing Your Project

## Goal
You will identify the most important parts of your "dog smelling station" project to talk about in your presentation.

## Preparation
*   Open your Canva project for the dog smelling station.
*   Have a piece of paper or a digital note ready to write down your notes.

## Instructions
Follow each step below. Mark the checkbox when you are done with that step.

1. Open your "dog smelling station" project in Canva.
   - [ ] Done

2. Look at Slide 1.
   - [ ] Done

3. Write one sentence about Slide 1 using this frame:
   *Slide 1 shows ___________________.*
   - [ ] Done

4. Look at the next slide.
   - [ ] Done

5. Write one sentence about that slide using this frame:
   *This slide shows ___________________.*
   - [ ] Done

6. Repeat steps 4 and 5 for every other slide in your project until you have finished all of them.
   - [ ] Done

7. Look at all the sentences you wrote. Pick the **3** best ones to be your main points.
   - [ ] Done

## Finish Line
You are finished when you have one sentence written for every slide and have picked your 3 favorite parts.
