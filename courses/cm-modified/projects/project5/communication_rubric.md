# Communication Rubric: Dog Smelling Station Presentation

## Goal
This rubric checks how well you share your ideas with others during your presentation.

## Preparation Checklist (L3)
To get a high score, check off every box in this list for your presentation.

### 1. Presentation Structure
You have organized your main points clearly.
- [ ] Your presentation focuses on **three** main parts:
  *   The Hook (Introduction)
  *   The Main Idea (The Dog Smelling Station)
  *   The Call to Action (What people can do next)

### 2. Visual Clarity
Your Canva slides are easy for everyone to see and understand.
- [ ] **Font Size:** All text is large enough to read from a distance.
- [ ] **Labels:** Every picture or image has a clear label telling the audience what it is.
- [ ] **Spell Check:** There are no spelling mistakes in your slide text.

### 3. Presentation Readiness
You have everything you need to speak clearly.
- [ ] **Script Check:** You have a written script for each of your three main points.
- [ ] **Question Card:** You have prepared one "Question Card" to help you if someone asks you a question at the end.

## Final Review
Your presentation is ready when all boxes above are checked and your slides look clear and professional.
