# Teacher's Guide: Dog Smelling Station Project

## Overview
This three-day project is designed for a student on an adjusted curriculum track. The goal is to prepare a presentation for a "dog smelling station" project using Canva. 

The materials are structured to provide high levels of support while maintaining the cognitive challenge of the task. We have removed all requirements for inference, ensuring that every step is explicit and provides a clear path to success.

## Alignment with Proficiency Scales
This project specifically targets **L3** on both the **Creativity** and **Communication** scales:

*   **Creativity (L3):** Instead of asking the student to "brainstorm" or "innovate" from scratch, the student selects key components from their existing Canva work. The use of sentence frames ensures they can articulate *why* these parts are important without the barrier of complex sentence construction.
*   **Communication (L3):** Rather than a spontaneous speech, the student follows a structured path to prepare a script and a polished visual aid. This includes clear "checkpoints" for font size, labels, and spelling.

## The Path to Success: Mapping Lessons to Rubrics

The three-day plan acts as a scaffold that builds toward the final rubrics:

### Day 1: Selection & Focus (Creativity Foundation)
*   **Focus:** Identifying the "meat" of the project.
*   **Support:** Instead of open-ended exploration, the student identifies specific slides in their existing Canva work.
*   **Link to Rubric:** This directly prepares them for the **Creativity L3** requirement of selecting a focused idea and explaining its purpose using frames.

### Day 2: Scripting & Structure (Communication Foundation)
*   **Focus:** Translating visual ideas into spoken words.
*   **Support:** Sentence frames (e.g., *"I want to tell my audience about..."*) are provided for every section. This removes the "blank page" problem while requiring the student to perform the actual mental work of deciding what is important.
*   **Link to Rubric:** This builds the foundation for **Communication L3**, ensuring they have a prepared script and a clear three-part structure (Hook, Idea, Call to Action).

### Day 3: Polish & Practice (Final Verification)
*   **Focus:** Ensuring professional quality and confidence.
*   **Support:** A checklist ensures that technical requirements—like label placement and font size—are met.
*   **Link to Rubric:** This finalizes the **Communication L3** requirements, specifically the "polished" aspects of the presentation (labels, grammar, and script readiness).

## Facilitator Tips
1.  **Wait Time:** Allow the student time to fill in the blanks in the sentence frames. They are doing the heavy lifting of thought; the frame is just the vehicle.
2.  **Literal Interpretation:** If a student seems confused, it is likely because they are trying to "infer" what comes next. Refer them back to the **Finish Line** at the bottom of each day's sheet—it defines exactly when they are done with that step.
3.  **Positive Reinforcement:** Use the "Supportive but Direct" tone as outlined in the student context. Celebrate their completion of each checkmark on the list.
