## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Part A — Why Does This Problem Exist?

You are going to look at the problem from three levels: the big picture, the community, and the individual.

**Step 1.** Fill in the table below.

Look back at your research notes from Checkpoint 2 if you need help.

| Level | What this level means | What is happening at this level in your topic |
|---|---|---|
| Big picture (systems and laws) | Large-scale causes — things like government policy, global trends, or how society is set up | |
| Community level | What is happening in neighborhoods, schools, workplaces, or local groups | |
| Individual level | What happens to a single person in their daily life | |

**Here is an example of a completed table (topic: high blood pressure):**

| Level | What this level means | What is happening |
|---|---|---|
| Big picture | Large-scale causes | Food companies use a lot of salt in processed foods, and there are few regulations about it |
| Community level | Local groups and neighborhoods | In many neighborhoods, it is cheaper and easier to buy fast food than fresh vegetables |
| Individual level | One person's daily life | A person may not know they have high blood pressure because there are often no symptoms |

---

### Part B — Who Does This Problem Affect?

**Step 2.** Answer these two questions.

> **Question 1:** Who is most affected by this problem?
> Write one sentence:
> ___________________________________________

> **Question 2:** Who else is affected, even if they are not the ones with the problem?
> Write one sentence:
> ___________________________________________

**Here is an example:**

> Question 1: People with high blood pressure, especially adults over 40, are most affected because they face serious health risks like heart attacks and strokes.
> Question 2: Their family members are also affected because they may have to help care for them or worry about their health.

---

### Part C — What Is Already Being Done?

**Step 3.** Look back at your research sources from Checkpoint 2.

Find one thing that is already being done to help with this problem.

**Step 4.** Fill in the frame below:

> One thing people are already doing to help with [TOPIC] is: ___________________________________________
>
> This helps because: ___________________________________________
>
> One thing that is NOT yet solved is: ___________________________________________

**Here is an example:**

> One thing people are already doing to help with high blood pressure is: encouraging people to exercise more and reduce salt in their diet.
>
> This helps because: lifestyle changes can lower blood pressure without medication for some people.
>
> One thing that is NOT yet solved is: many people do not get regular checkups, so they don't know their blood pressure is high until it becomes dangerous.

---

### Part D — What Should Your Canva Design Focus On?

Now that you understand the problem more deeply, you need to decide what your Canva design will say.

**Step 5.** Choose one focus for your Canva design from the options below.

Put a checkmark next to the one you pick.

- [ ] What the problem is and why it matters
- [ ] Who the problem affects and how
- [ ] What people can do to help or protect themselves
- [ ] What is already being done and what still needs to change
- [ ] My own idea (describe it here): ___________________________________________

**Step 6.** Fill in this sentence:

> My Canva design will focus on: ___________________________________________
>
> I chose this because it is the most useful thing for [person's name] to know: ___________________________________________
