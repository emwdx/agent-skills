# Project 4 Brief — Talk, Research, Create

## Student

This project is designed for a Grade 10 student at Nido de Aguilas enrolled in Changemakers, working on a solo, modified curriculum track.

---

## Project Description

In Project 3, you chose a topic you were curious about and became an expert through research. In Project 4, you are going to start with a person instead of a topic.

You will talk to someone you know — a family member, a neighbor, or a family friend — and find out about a challenge they face in their life. Then you will research that challenge. At the end of the project, you will create something in Canva that helps that person understand the problem better.

This project is about making something useful for a real person, based on a real conversation.

---

## What You Will Do

**Checkpoint 1 — Choose Your Person and Topic** (Days 1–2)
You will pick one person you know and have a short conversation with them about a challenge they face. You will use a structured guide to have the conversation and write down what you learned.

**Checkpoint 2 — Research the Challenge** (Days 3–4)
You will research the challenge your person described. You will use websites and public information to learn the facts about the problem.

**Checkpoint 3 — Understand the Problem** (Days 5–6)
You will go deeper. You will look at why the problem exists, who it affects, and what is already being done about it.

**Checkpoint 4 — Create in Canva** (Days 7–8)
You will plan and build a Canva design — a poster or infographic — that helps your person understand the challenge better. You will make at least two changes to improve it.

**Checkpoint 5 — Share and Reflect** (Days 9–10)
You will share your Canva design with your person and with your class. You will give feedback to one classmate and write a short reflection on your own work.

---

## Tools You Will Use

- Canva (for your final design)
- Gemini or other AI tools (for research support, if needed)
- Public websites, articles, and reports (for research)

---

## What Makes This Different from Project 3

In Project 3, you chose a topic you were already interested in. In Project 4, you start with someone else's experience and let that guide what you learn. You are not just becoming an expert — you are using what you learn to help a real person.

---

10

## Output Needed

A Markdown file for each of five checkpoints. Each file has explicit steps, sentence frames, and a completion checklist. Assessments use the adapted proficiency scales.
