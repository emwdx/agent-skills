# Project 4 — Day 0: What Is This Project?

---

## What You Are Going to Do

In Project 3, you picked a topic you were curious about and became an expert on it.

In Project 4, you are going to start with a person instead of a topic.

Here is how the project works:

1. You will choose one person in your life — someone you know — who has a challenge or a problem.
2. You will have a short conversation with that person about their challenge.
3. You will do some research to learn more about that challenge.
4. You will make something in Canva — a poster or an infographic — that helps that person understand the problem better.
5. You will share what you made with that person and with your class.

That's the whole project. You start with a person. You end with something useful for them.

---

## What Is Canva?

Canva is a free design tool you can use in a browser. You do not need to know how to code. You pick a template, replace the text with your own information, and customize the colors and images.

Your final product will be a Canva poster or infographic — one page that explains your topic in a clear, visual way.

---

## Today's Job: Start Thinking About Your Person

Before you can do anything else in this project, you need to choose one person to talk to.

That person needs to be:

- Someone you know in real life (not a celebrity or a public figure)
- Someone you can actually have a conversation with
- Someone who has a challenge or a problem you have noticed or heard about

They do not need to have a huge, world-changing problem. It can be something real and ordinary — something they deal with at work, at home, or in their health, neighborhood, or daily life.
