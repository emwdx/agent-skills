# Checkpoint 5 — Share and Reflect

**Focus:** Collaboration — Contributing to the Class Community
**Proficiency Target:** Collaboration L3

---

## What You Are Doing

You have built a Canva design for a real person about a real challenge.

In this last checkpoint, you will do three things.

You will share your design with your person. You will give feedback to one classmate during the class showcase. And you will write a short reflection on your own project.

---

## Class Days for This Checkpoint: Days 9–10

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Part A — Share with Your Person

**Step 1.** Show your Canva design to the person you talked to in Checkpoint 1.

This can happen in person, by sending them the file, or by showing them on a screen.

**Step 2.** Ask them one question after they see it:

> "Is there anything here that surprises you or that you want to know more about?"

**Step 3.** Write down what they said:

> When I showed my design to [person's name], they said: ___________________________________________

**Here is an example:**

> When I showed my design to my uncle Marco, he said: "I didn't know so many people have this. I thought it was just me."

---

### Part B — Gallery Walk Feedback

During the class showcase, you will look at one other student's project.

**Step 4.** Walk to one classmate's screen during the showcase.

**Step 5.** Look at their project for two minutes.

**Step 6.** Fill in the feedback frame below.

> One thing I noticed about this student's design is: ___________________________________________
>
> One design choice they made that was different from mine is: ___________________________________________
>
> One question I have about their project is: ___________________________________________

**Here is an example of a completed frame:**

> One thing I noticed about this student's design is: they used a lot of photos instead of icons, which made it feel more real.
>
> One design choice they made that was different from mine is: they organized their sections in a timeline instead of separate boxes.
>
> One question I have about their project is: how did they decide which photos to use?

**Step 7.** Share your feedback with that student, in writing or by telling them.

---

### Part C — Self-Reflection

**Step 8.** Read your work from all five checkpoints before you write your reflection.

**Step 9.** Fill in the reflection frame below.

> One goal I had for this project was: ___________________________________________
>
> I think I met / did not meet that goal because: ___________________________________________
>
> The part of this project I am most proud of is: ___________________________________________
>
> One thing I would do differently next time is: ___________________________________________
>
> One thing I learned about [TOPIC] that I did not know before is: ___________________________________________

**Here is an example of a completed reflection:**

> One goal I had for this project was: to make something that my uncle Marco would actually find useful.
>
> I think I met that goal because: when I showed him the design, he said he learned something new and asked to keep a copy.
>
> The part of this project I am most proud of is: the section on what people can do, because I made the steps really clear and specific.
>
> One thing I would do differently next time is: add more visuals in the first section — it has too much text.
>
> One thing I learned about high blood pressure that I did not know before is: that it often has no symptoms, which is why it is called the "silent killer."

---

## You Are Done When:

- [ ] You have shown your design to your person and written down what they said in Step 3.
- [ ] You have filled in all three blanks in the gallery walk feedback frame in Step 6.
- [ ] You have shared your feedback with that classmate.
- [ ] You have filled in all five blanks in the reflection frame in Step 9.

---

## Proficiency Check — Collaboration L3

Your teacher will use these things to check your work:

1. You filled in your reflection log showing what your goal was and whether you met it.
2. You gave one piece of written feedback to a classmate using the provided sentence frame.
3. You described one personal goal from the project.

If your reflection frame has all five blanks filled in with specific information, and your gallery walk feedback is complete, you are at L3.
