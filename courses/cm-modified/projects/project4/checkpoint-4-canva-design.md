# Checkpoint 4 — Create in Canva

**Topic:** [TOPIC — fill in after teacher confirms in Checkpoint 1]
**Focus:** Creativity and Communication — Building Something for a Real Audience
**Proficiency Target:** Creativity L3 and Communication L3

---

## What You Are Doing

You are going to build a Canva design that helps your person understand the challenge you researched.

Your design could be a poster or an infographic. It should include facts from your research, organized in a way that is easy to read.

You will plan it first, then build it in Canva, then make two improvements.

---

## Class Days for This Checkpoint: Days 7–8

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Part A — Plan Your Design

Before you open Canva, decide what your design will look like.

**Step 1.** Choose one design type from the list below.

Put a checkmark next to the one you pick.

- [ ] An infographic (facts and numbers arranged with visuals)
- [ ] A poster (a main message with supporting details)
- [ ] A one-page guide (step-by-step information for your person)
- [ ] My own idea (describe it here): ___________________________________________

**Step 2.** Fill in this planning frame:

> My design is called: ___________________________________________
>
> My design will help [person's name] understand: ___________________________________________
>
> When they look at it, they will learn: ___________________________________________

**Here is an example:**

> My design is called: What You Need to Know About High Blood Pressure
>
> My design will help my uncle Marco understand: what high blood pressure is, why it matters, and three things he can do about it
>
> When he looks at it, he will learn: that high blood pressure is common, that it often has no symptoms, and that diet and exercise can make a real difference

---

### Part B — Plan Your Sections

Your design needs at least three sections. Each section has a heading and some information.

**Step 3.** Fill in the section planner below.

| Section | Heading (what you will write at the top) | What information will go here |
|---|---|---|
| Section 1 | | |
| Section 2 | | |
| Section 3 | | |

**Here is an example of a completed planner:**

| Section | Heading | What information will go here |
|---|---|---|
| Section 1 | What is high blood pressure? | A short explanation of what it is and how common it is |
| Section 2 | Why does it matter? | Facts about the health risks — heart attack, stroke |
| Section 3 | What can you do? | Three specific steps: reduce salt, exercise, get checkups |

---

### Part C — Build in Canva

**Step 4.** Open Canva at canva.com.

**Step 5.** Click "Create a design."

**Step 6.** Choose a template type.

In the search bar, type one of these:
- "infographic"
- "health poster"
- "awareness poster"
- "one-page flyer"

**Step 7.** Choose a template that looks like it could work for your topic.

**Step 8.** Replace the template text with your own information.

Use your section planner from Step 3.

For each section:
- Replace the heading with your heading.
- Replace the body text with your own facts from your research.

**Step 9.** Add at least one image or icon that matches your topic.

(Canva has a library of free images and icons. Click the Elements tab to find them.)

**Step 10.** Check the design checklist below before moving on.

**Design Checklist:**

- [ ] The text is large enough to read easily
- [ ] There are at least three sections with headings
- [ ] Every fact comes from your research (nothing made up)
- [ ] There is at least one image or icon
- [ ] Your name is somewhere on the design

---

### Part D — Improve Your Design

After you finish the first version, you will make two improvements. Each improvement is called an iteration.

**Step 11.** Look at your design and find one thing to change.

Fill in the Iteration Log:

| Iteration | What I changed | Why I changed it | What it looks like after the change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | |
| 2 | | | |

**Here is an example of a completed row:**

| Iteration | What I changed | Why I changed it | What it looks like after the change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Made the headings bigger and bolder | They were hard to see against the background | The headings now stand out clearly |

**Step 12.** Make your first change in Canva.

**Step 13.** Fill in Row 1 of the Iteration Log.

**Step 14.** Make your second change in Canva.

**Step 15.** Fill in Row 2 of the Iteration Log.

---

### Part E — Export Your Design

**Step 16.** Click "Share" in the top right corner of Canva.

**Step 17.** Click "Download."

**Step 18.** Choose "PDF Standard" or "PNG" as the file type.

**Step 19.** Download the file to your computer.

**Step 20.** Share the file with your teacher.

## Proficiency Check — Creativity L3 and Communication L3

**Creativity:** Your teacher will look for:

1. You selected a design idea and completed the planning template explaining what it is and who it helps.
2. You built a design using a step-by-step checklist (this document).
3. You made two documented improvements using the Iteration Log.

**Communication:** Your teacher will look for:

1. Your design has a clear main message (readable heading or title).
2. The information is organized in sections that are easy to follow.
3. You completed the design checklist including font size, images, and grammar.

**Bonus sentence frame — fill this in after your two iterations:**

> One way I could still improve this design is: ___________________________________________

If your design has three sections, all five checklist items are checked, and your Iteration Log has two complete rows, you are at L3 for both competencies.
