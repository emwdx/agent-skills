# Checkpoint 2 — Research the Challenge

**Topic:** [TOPIC — fill in after teacher confirms in Checkpoint 1]
**Focus:** Critical Thinking — Finding and Using Evidence
**Proficiency Target:** Critical Thinking L3

---

## What You Are Doing

In Checkpoint 1, you learned about a challenge from a real person.

Now you are going to research that challenge. You will use websites and public information to find out what experts say about the problem.

You will fill in a research chart and write one paragraph explaining what you found.

### Part D — Write Your Summary Paragraph

**Step 9.** Read back through your notes from Parts B and C.

**Step 10.** Write one paragraph using the frame below.

Fill in each blank with your own words.

> The challenge I am researching is ___________________________.
> According to [Source 1 name], ___________________________.
> According to [Source 2 name], ___________________________.
> One thing I still want to understand better is ___________________________.

**Here is an example of a completed paragraph:**

> The challenge I am researching is high blood pressure.
> According to the CDC, high blood pressure affects nearly half of all adults in the United States, and many people do not know they have it.
> According to the World Health Organization, lifestyle changes like reducing salt and getting more exercise can help bring blood pressure down.
> One thing I still want to understand better is why some people can manage it with lifestyle changes while others need medication.

## Proficiency Check — Critical Thinking L3

Your teacher will use these things to check your work:

1. You selected one claim from your reading and matched it to supporting evidence from one of your sources.
2. You completed a sentence stem describing one thing you still do not know.
3. You wrote a paragraph with two specific pieces of evidence from your sources.

If the Part D paragraph has specific facts or details from your sources (not just general statements), you are at L3.
