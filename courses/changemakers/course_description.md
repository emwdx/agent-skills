# Changemakers Course Overview

Changemakers is an intentional, design-thinking-influenced course for Grade 10 students at our international school in Santiago. The course isn't a generic leadership seminar or a business incubator; it is a journey into the work of creating meaningful change within communities.

## Core Focus
Students learn to navigate the complexities of community engagement by focusing on several core pillars:
*   **Empathy & Research:** Moving beyond assumptions to listen deeply and gather evidence before seeking solutions.
*   **Iteration:** Embracing the "messy" middle of project development where ideas are tested, refined, and pivoted.
*   **Communication & Advocacy:** Crafting clear messages for specific audiences and moving from awareness-raising toward concrete action.
*   **Collaboration:** Building projects that require diverse voices, shared accountability, and evidence of collective work.

## Course Structure
The course is structured in two distinct halves to balance support with autonomy:

### Semester 1: Foundational Skills
In the first semester, we focus on building a shared vocabulary and toolkit. Students engage in structured projects designed to develop their skills in research, prototyping, and collaboration. We move methodically through defined phases, ensuring every student has a firm foundation before moving into independent work.

### Semester 2: Autonomy & Innovation
In the second semester, students with demonstrated proficiency transition toward more open-ended projects. While they still have access to our shared framework and resources, they enjoy greater autonomy in choosing their specific topics, project formats, and pathways for advocacy.

## The Changemakers Approach
We believe that real change requires more than just passion; it requires a commitment to the process. We prioritize:
*   **Evidence over Opinion:** Moving students away from "what we think" toward "what the data shows."
*   **Actionable Advocacy:** Ensuring our efforts reach specific audiences with clear calls to action.
*   **Deliberate Growth:** Providing a "defined hybrid path"—a structured journey that gives students space to make their own decisions within a supported framework.

## Collaborative Environment
This course is co-taught. We work as a team to guide students through the hurdles of design thinking, ensuring they have the support needed to move from initial curiosity to impactful reality.
